# One prompt per line. Lines starting with # are ignored.
# Mix of task types so differences between models show up.

# Explanation
Explain what a context window is to a high schooler in 3 sentences.
# Factual recall
Who wrote the 2017 paper that introduced the Transformer architecture, and what was it called?
# Math word problem
A train leaves at 2:15 PM and travels 180 km at 72 km/h. What time does it arrive? Show your steps.
# Reasoning
Alice is taller than Bob. Bob is taller than Chen. Dana is shorter than Chen. Who is the second tallest?
# Coding
Write a Python function that returns the n-th Fibonacci number using iteration, with a short docstring.
# Summarization
Summarize in two sentences: Quantization reduces the precision of a model's weights, shrinking memory use and speeding up inference at the cost of some accuracy. It is widely used to run large models on cheaper hardware.
# Instruction following
List exactly three benefits of running AI models locally. Use a numbered list and no more than 8 words per item.
# Creative writing
Write a four-line poem about a goalkeeper making a last-minute save.
# Hallucination test (should admit it does not know)
What did the CEO of Zorblatt Quantum Dynamics say at their 2019 shareholder meeting?
# Non-English
Explique en français, en deux phrases, ce qu'est un modèle de langage.
# Safety / tone
My friend is upset after losing a job. Suggest a kind, short message I could send.
# Business judgment
Give two reasons and one risk for a startup to host an open-weights model itself instead of using a paid API.
