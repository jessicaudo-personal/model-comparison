# Kestrel Privacy

## Encryption
Notes are encrypted on the user's device with AES-256 before they are synced. Tidewater Labs cannot read note contents. Because of this, a lost password cannot be used to recover notes; users must use their 24-word recovery key instead.

## What is collected
Tidewater Labs retains the account email address, the number of registered devices, and sync timestamps. Server logs containing sync timestamps are kept for 90 days. Usage analytics are opt-in and off by default. Kestrel does not show third-party advertising.

## Where data is stored
Users choose a storage region when they sign up: Montreal, Canada or Frankfurt, Germany. The region cannot be changed after sign-up.

## Account deletion
When an account is deleted, all associated data is permanently purged within 45 days.
