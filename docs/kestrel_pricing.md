# Kestrel Pricing

## Plans
Kestrel has three plans: Free, Plus, and Team.

The Free plan includes up to 500 notes and syncs across 2 devices.

The Plus plan costs $6 per month, or $60 per year when billed annually. It includes unlimited notes and syncing across up to 5 devices.

The Team plan costs $12 per user per month and requires a minimum of 3 users. It adds a shared admin console and single sign-on (SSO).

## Discounts and trials
Students with a valid .edu email address receive 40% off the Plus plan. New users can try Plus free for 14 days.

## Refunds
Annual Plus subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly subscriptions are not refunded, but can be cancelled at any time.
